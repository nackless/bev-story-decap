Writing blog posts in Obsidian is now completely seamless. When you press Enter in Obsidian to create space between paragraphs, Astro renders that exact clean spacing automatically on your site.

Every paragraph is spaced out comfortably for reading, ensuring long-form articles look elegant without any manual formatting hacks.
