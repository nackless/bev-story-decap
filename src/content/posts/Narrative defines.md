The world as we see it today is nothing but the outcome of a infinite number of dynamic things and / or coincidences that happen every moment. A infinite things that can go terribly wrong but somehow we have still managed to exist in a bubble that is but a flutter in what we think is a vast universe, which we don't know much about but certainly is so ancient than we can't ever imagine. 

However, that's not the point. This post is more about us, humans, for our miraculous existence, for the fact that we have been able to exist and thrive, propagate information and create narratives that have shaped us and life on this planet.

I came across this quote and it felt very apt.

> [!quote] “We are an impossibility in an impossible universe.”
>
> — Ray Bradbury

It is brilliant but also impossible. We have social media today and it's easy to have a presence which the whole planet can feel in theory but not everyone is heard. Historically, we know a handful of names who have been the centre of culture.

That brings me to  the final bit. Who defines the narrative? Is it something that develops automatically because the world is experiencing things and changing or is it a selected few who know how to shape the world. I am not convinced that it is completely automatic as that would be too much of a coincidence to be true but I also feel that no one person or power can decide this. I'm pretty sure that there is a self driven mode but the steering does need a bit of a nudge now and then to keep things on track to some kind of eventually that we don't know about.

Majority of us, or maybe all of us think we are doing things that we decide, but if you stop to think, is this actually the case? We are nothing, but an outcome of all the data we are fed since the day we are born. Defining wrong and right is more a matter of thinking what is wrong and what is right than actually being wrong or right. Once upon a time, murder was not considered a crime after all. In fact, even in today's world, the powerful can get away with murder and crime but a common man is put behind the bars for raising a finger.

I want to have a say, but I need to understand that it might not actually be my say but my internal programming has happened in a way that, this is what I'm going to be doing.

The point, who defines the narrative. I know it is not all black and white, and there are more shades of grey that 50. Way more. In theory, I understand this question can have many answers and there is no way anyone can pinpoint all the answers but I do feel strongly about a few things and hence, this post.

In essence what I think; Learn to control the narrative and you will be the winner.
 

I didn't see Jesus, or Allah or any of the Gods but they are the most influential voices the world has heard or believes in. Who created them? For that matter, even science, what is evidence based is what is backed by money. What is not funded will not ever be mainstream.

All of what is written and marketed propagates, eventually becomes the truth. And the history books are written by those who win and so we see the side of world that persisted and the other side, we know what we think is correct but I have my doubts.

But who defines the narrative. And who can.

We, as humans, start to believe in what is fed to us. Today, I question religion and the concept of God but not so long ago, it was considered to be a sin and I might even be burnt alive for even saying something on these lines. What changed? Science? Maybe but also the thinking of the whole society. It was the people im power who said, ok.
