The world as we see it today is nothing but the outcome of a infinite number of dynamic things and / or coincidences that happen every moment. A infinite things that can go terribly wrong but somehow we have still managed to exist in a bubble that is but a flutter in what we think is a vast universe, which we don't know much about but certainly is so ancient than we can't ever imagine. 

However, that's not the point. This post is more about us, humans, for our miraculous existence, for the fact that we have been able to exist and thrive, propagate information and create narratives that have shaped us and life on this planet.

I came across this quote and it felt very apt.

> [!quote] “We are an impossibility in an impossible universe.”
>
> — Ray Bradbury

I is brilliant but also impossible. We have social media today and it's easy to have a presence which the whole planet can feel in theory but not everyone is heard. Historically, we know a handful of names who have been the centre of culture.

Majority of us, or maybe all of us think we are doing things that we decide, but if you stop to think, is this actually the case? We are nothing, but an outcome of all the data we are fed since the day we are born. Defining wrong and right is more a matter of thinking what is wrong and what is right than actually being wrong or right. Once upon a time, murder was not considered a crime after all. In fact, even in today's world, the powerful can get away with murder and crime but a common man is put behind the bars for raising a finger.

Learn to control the narrative and you will be the winnerl 

My point is, it is not all black and white, and there are more shades of grey that 50. Way more. 

I didn't see Jesus, or Allah or any of the Gods but they are the most influential voices the world has heard or is told so. Who created them? For that matter, even science, what is evidence based is what is financially backed. Who will fund you if you are not profitable, well, no one.

My point, it is what is written and propagated, that becomes the truth. And the history books are written by those who win and those who know that will be the definition of future. 

But who defines the narrative. And who can.
