The world as we see it today is nothing but the outcome of a infinite number of dynamic things and / or coincidences that happen every moment. A infinite things that can go terribly wrong but somehow we have still managed to exist in a bubble that is but a flutter in what we think is a vast universe, which we don't know much about but certainly is so ancient than we can't ever imagine. 

However, that's not the point. This post is more about us, humans, for our miraculous existence, for the fact that we have been able to exist and thrive, propagate information and create narratives that have shaped us and life on this planet.

I just find it so surreal. But also amazing. We have social media today and it's easy to have a presence which the whole planet can feel in theory but not everyone is heard. It's a choice.

Majority of us, or maybe all of us think we are doing things that we decide but if you still to think, you will know that is such a lie. We are nothing but a outcome of a million things that are fed to our senses since the day we are born. Defining wrong and right is more a matter of thinking what is wrong and what is right than actually being wrong or right.

Learn to control the narrative and you will be the winner
